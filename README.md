# GolpoNeer — গল্পনীড়

*A nest for stories.*

**Live: https://golponeer.com**

GolpoNeer is a calm home for reading and publishing stories in Bangla and English, chapter by chapter. No noise, no clutter — just stories, presented the way they deserve to be read.

Previously called GolpoGhor — renamed GolpoNeer in October 2026. (The repository kept its original name.)

## For readers

- **37 published stories, 510 chapters, over 1.1 million words** — and growing.
- **Bangla classics and English classics side by side** — Tagore, Sarat Chandra, and Bankim alongside Alice, Oz, Jekyll and Hyde, and more, plus original stories from new writers.
- **Discover by mood and genre** — Horror, Mystery, Fantasy, Romance, History, Science Fiction, and more, with shelves for new arrivals, featured stories, and quick reads.
- **A reader that stays out of the way** — themes, font sizes, and spacing you can adjust to your eyes, and your place saved as you go.
- **Keepsakes** — save a line you love as a beautiful card to keep or share.
- **Follow writers, keep a reading list, and pick up where you left off.**

## For writers

- **Write and publish chapter by chapter**, in Bangla or English, from a simple dashboard and editor with autosave.
- **Your stories, your readers** — readers can follow you, read along as you publish, and save lines from your stories as keepsakes.
- **We're opening up more widely in December** — if you write, come read first, and watch this space.

## Right now

GolpoNeer is live and open to readers. New stories are published in verified batches, and the catalogue keeps growing. The focus is simple: a clean, calm environment that is easy to use, with nothing complicated in the way of the stories.

## How it's built

## For developers

Built with React + Vite + TypeScript + Tailwind CSS + Supabase (Postgres, Auth, Storage). Technical setup and architecture notes live in `docs/development.md`. Run it locally with `npm install` and `npm run dev` — demo mode needs no credentials.
